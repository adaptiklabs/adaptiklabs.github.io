Adaptik Icons

A lightweight Material Design icon pack that gives your home screen a clean, consistent look.

Coming soon to Google Play

Features

  • Material Design Built with Material 3, including dynamic colour on Android 12 and newer.
  • Themed icons Adaptive icons with monochrome versions for themed icons on supported launchers.
  • Launcher support Works with Nova, Lawnchair, and most other launchers that support icon packs.
  • Search and browse Find any icon quickly with built-in search.
  • Icon requests Missing an icon? Send a request by email straight from the app.
  • No tracking No account, no analytics, and no ads.

How to apply

Adaptik Icons is an icon pack. Your launcher applies it — the app does not change home-screen icons by itself.

  1. Install Adaptik Icons from Google Play.
  2. Use a launcher that supports icon packs (for example Nova Launcher or Lawnchair).
  3. Open that launcher's settings, find Icon pack (or Icon theme), and select Adaptik Icons.

Examples

  • Lawnchair Home Settings → General → Icon pack
  • Nova Launcher Nova Settings → Look & feel → Icon theme
  • Stock launchers Pixel, OnePlus, Samsung, and similar often do not support third-party icon packs. Use Nova or Lawnchair instead.
